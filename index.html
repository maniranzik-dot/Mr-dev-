Unit 5: Thales Theorem

Meaning of Thales Theorem

Thales theorem states that an angle formed in a semicircle is always a right angle (90°).

Properties

  • The diameter of a circle forms the longest chord.
  • The angle in a semicircle is 90°.
  • It is used to solve problems involving circles and triangles.

Example

If AB is a diameter of a circle and C is a point on the circle:
∠ACB = 90°

Unit 6: Pythagoras Theorem

Theorem

In a right angled triangle, the square of the longest side equals the sum of squares of the other two sides.

a² + b² = c²

  • a and b are shorter sides.
  • c is the hypotenuse (longest side).

Example

A triangle has sides 3cm and 4cm.
c² = 3² + 4²
c² = 9 + 16
c² = 25
c = 5cm

Unit 7: Vectors

Meaning of Vector

A vector is a quantity that has both magnitude and direction.

Examples of Vectors

  • Displacement
  • Velocity
  • Force

Vector Notation

Vectors are represented using arrows.
Example: AB⃗

Operations on Vectors

  • Addition of vectors
  • Subtraction of vectors
  • Multiplication by a scalar

Unit 8: Parallel and Perpendicular Projections

Parallel Projection

A projection where lines drawn from points are parallel to each other.

Orthogonal (Perpendicular) Projection

A projection where projectors meet the object at 90°.

Properties

  • Used in technical drawing.
  • Shows accurate shape and size.
  • Helps represent 3D objects in 2D.

Applications

  • Engineering drawings
  • Architecture
  • Design
