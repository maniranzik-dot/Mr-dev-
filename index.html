📖 Unit 10: Magnetism

1. Meaning of Magnetism

Magnetism is the property of a material that allows it to attract or repel other magnetic materials.

2. Magnets

A magnet is a material that produces a magnetic field and attracts magnetic substances.

3. Magnetic Materials

Magnetic materials are substances that can be attracted by a magnet.

Examples:

  • Iron
  • Steel
  • Nickel
  • Cobalt

4. Non-magnetic Materials

These are materials that are not attracted by magnets.

Examples:
  • Wood
  • Plastic
  • Glass
  • Rubber

5. Types of Magnets

(a) Permanent Magnets

Magnets that retain their magnetism for a long time.

Examples:
  • Bar magnet
  • Horse-shoe magnet

(b) Temporary Magnets

Magnets that lose magnetism easily.

Example:
  • Soft iron magnet

6. Magnetic Poles

Every magnet has two poles:

  • North pole (N)
  • South pole (S)

7. Law of Magnetic Poles

  • Like poles repel each other.
  • Unlike poles attract each other.

8. Magnetic Field

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet where magnetic forces can be detected.

9. Magnetic Field Lines

  • They show the direction of magnetic force.
  • They move from North pole to South pole outside a magnet.
  • They never cross each other.

10. Methods of Making Magnets

(a) Stroking Method

A magnet is rubbed repeatedly in one direction along a magnetic material.

(b) Electrical Method

An electric current is passed through a coil around a magnetic material.

11. Electromagnet

An electromagnet is a temporary magnet produced when electric current flows through a coil of wire around an iron core.

Factors Affecting Strength of Electromagnets

  • Number of turns of the coil.
  • Amount of current flowing.
  • Type of core material.

12. Applications of Magnetism

  • Electric motors.
  • Loudspeakers.
  • Magnetic door locks.
  • Electric bells.
  • Magnetic separation in industries.

💡 Examples

1. A compass uses a magnet to show direction.

2. An electric bell uses an electromagnet to produce sound.

3. A crane uses electromagnets to lift iron materials.
