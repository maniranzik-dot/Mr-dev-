Unit 14: Reflection of Light in Lenses

Reflection is the bouncing back of light when it strikes a surface.

Laws of Reflection

  • Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.
  • The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in the same plane.
Angle of incidence (i) = Angle of reflection (r)

Lenses

A lens is a transparent material that refracts light to form images.

  • Convex lens: converges light rays.
  • Concave lens: diverges light rays.
