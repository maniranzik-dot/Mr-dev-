📖 Unit 7: Simple Machines

1. Meaning of a Simple Machine

A simple machine is a device that makes work easier by changing the size or direction of a force.

2. Importance of Simple Machines

  • Reduce the effort needed to do work.
  • Change the direction of an applied force.
  • Help people perform tasks more easily.

3. Types of Simple Machines

(a) Lever

A lever is a rigid bar that turns around a fixed point called a fulcrum.

Parts of a Lever:

  • Fulcrum - the pivot point.
  • Effort - the force applied.
  • Load - the weight being moved.

Classes of Levers

First class lever:

Fulcrum is between effort and load.

Examples:
  • Scissors
  • Seesaw
  • Pliers

Second class lever:

Load is between fulcrum and effort.

Examples:
  • Wheelbarrow
  • Bottle opener

Third class lever:

Effort is between fulcrum and load.

Examples:
  • Tweezers
  • Human arm

4. Pulley

A pulley is a wheel with a groove that carries a rope or cable.

It is used to lift heavy loads and change the direction of force.

5. Wheel and Axle

A wheel and axle consists of a large wheel attached to a smaller axle.

Examples:
  • Door knob
  • Steering wheel

6. Inclined Plane

An inclined plane is a sloping surface used to raise or lower objects with less effort.

Examples:
  • Ramp
  • Sloping road

7. Mechanical Advantage (MA)

Mechanical advantage shows how much a machine multiplies the applied force.

Formula: MA = Load ÷ Effort

8. Velocity Ratio (VR)

Velocity ratio compares the distance moved by effort with the distance moved by load.

Formula: VR = Distance moved by effort ÷ Distance moved by load

9. Efficiency

Efficiency shows how well a machine converts input work into useful output work.

Formula: Efficiency = (MA ÷ VR) × 100%

10. Uses of Simple Machines

  • Lifting heavy loads.
  • Cutting materials.
  • Moving objects.
  • Reducing human effort.
`, examples:`

💡 Examples

1. A wheelbarrow is a second class lever.

2. If a machine lifts a load of 200 N using an effort of 50 N:

MA = 200 ÷ 50 = 4
