Senior Two Biology (Units 1-3)

UNIT 1: Classification of Living Organisms

Meaning of Classification

Classification is the process of grouping living organisms according to their similarities and differences.

Importance of Classification

  • Helps identification of organisms
  • Shows relationships between organisms
  • Makes study of organisms easier

Taxonomic Hierarchy

Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species

Five Kingdoms

  • Monera
  • Protoctista
  • Fungi
  • Plantae
  • Animalia

UNIT 2: Introduction to Environmental Biology

Environment

Environment is everything surrounding living organisms, including living and non-living factors.

Components of Environment

  • Biotic factors: plants, animals, microorganisms
  • Abiotic factors: water, soil, temperature, light

Ecosystem

An ecosystem is a community of organisms interacting with their physical environment.

UNIT 3: Passive Movement of Substances Across Cell Membrane

Diffusion

Diffusion is movement of particles from a region of high concentration to low concentration.

Osmosis

Osmosis is movement of water molecules through a selectively permeable membrane.

Factors affecting diffusion

  • Temperature
  • Concentration difference
  • Surface area
