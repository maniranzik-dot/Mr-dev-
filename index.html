Unit 13: Puberty and Sexual Maturation

1. Meaning of Puberty

Puberty is the stage of growth when a child develops into an adult capable of reproduction.

2. Changes During Puberty

Changes in Boys
  • Growth of facial and body hair.
  • Deepening of voice.
  • Growth of reproductive organs.
  • Production of sperm begins.
  • Increase in muscle development.
Changes in Girls
  • Development of breasts.
  • Growth of body hair.
  • Widening of hips.
  • Beginning of menstruation.
  • Release of eggs begins.

3. Hormones During Puberty

  • Testosterone - male sex hormone produced by testes.
  • Oestrogen - female sex hormone produced by ovaries.

4. Personal Hygiene During Puberty

  • Regular bathing.
  • Wearing clean clothes.
  • Eating a balanced diet.
  • Understanding body changes.

Unit 14: Reproduction, Pregnancy and Child Birth

1. Fertilisation

Fertilisation is the joining of the male gamete (sperm) and female gamete (egg) to form a zygote.

2. Development of Embryo

  • The zygote divides and forms an embryo.
  • The embryo implants in the uterus.
  • The foetus develops inside the uterus.

3. Pregnancy

Pregnancy is the period during which a baby develops inside the uterus of the mother.

Changes During Pregnancy

  • Increase in size of uterus.
  • Growth of the foetus.
  • Hormonal changes.
  • Increased nutritional needs.

4. Placenta

The placenta is an organ that connects the mother and foetus and allows exchange of materials.

Functions of Placenta

  • Provides oxygen to the foetus.
  • Provides nutrients.
  • Removes waste products.
  • Produces hormones needed for pregnancy.

5. Child Birth

Child birth is the process of delivering a baby from the uterus.

Stages of Child Birth

  • Opening of the cervix.
  • Delivery of the baby.
  • Removal of the placenta.

6. Care During Pregnancy

  • Eating a balanced diet.
  • Regular medical check-ups.
  • Avoiding harmful substances.
  • Getting enough rest.
