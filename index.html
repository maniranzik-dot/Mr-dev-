Unit 5: Measuring Liquid Pressure Using Manometer

A manometer is an instrument used to measure pressure of liquids and gases.

Pressure formula:
P = hρg

  • P = Pressure
  • h = Height of liquid column
  • ρ = Density of liquid
  • g = Gravitational acceleration

Unit 6: Application of Pascal's Principle

Pascal's principle states that pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.

Formula:
F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂

Applications

  • Hydraulic brakes
  • Hydraulic press
  • Hydraulic lift

Unit 7: Archimedes' Principle and Atmospheric Pressure

Archimedes' principle states that an object immersed in a fluid experiences an upward force equal to the weight of fluid displaced.

Upthrust = Weight of displaced fluid

Applications

  • Ships floating
  • Submarines
  • Hydrometers

Unit 8: Work, Power and Energy

Work is done when a force moves an object through a distance.

Formula:
Work = Force × Distance
W = F × d

Power is the rate of doing work.

Power = Work / Time

Energy is the ability to do work.

Types of energy

  • Kinetic energy
  • Potential energy
