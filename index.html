Unit 11: Basic Alternating Current Circuit

11.1 Alternating Current

Alternating current (AC) is an electric current that periodically changes its magnitude and direction.

11.2 Direct Current and Alternating Current

  • DC: Current flows in one direction.
  • AC: Current periodically changes direction.

11.3 AC Waveform

A simple alternating voltage or current can be represented by a sinusoidal wave.

V = V₀ sin(ωt)

Where V₀ is the peak voltage and ω is the angular frequency.

11.4 Frequency

Frequency is the number of complete cycles made per second. Its SI unit is hertz (Hz).

f = 1/T

where T is the period of the alternating current.

11.5 Period

The period is the time taken for one complete cycle.

T = 1/f

11.6 AC in Resistors

In a purely resistive AC circuit, voltage and current are in phase.

V = IR

11.7 Electrical Power in an AC Circuit

P = VI

For a purely resistive circuit, the average power can be expressed using RMS values.

P = VrmsIrms

11.8 Applications of AC

  • Domestic electricity supply
  • Electric motors
  • Transformers
  • Power transmission
  • Many household appliances

Unit 12: Reflection of Light

12.1 Light

Light is a form of electromagnetic radiation that can travel through a vacuum.

12.2 Reflection of Light

Reflection is the bouncing back of light into the same medium when light strikes a surface.

12.3 Laws of Reflection

  1. The angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection.
  2. The incident ray, reflected ray and the normal at the point of incidence lie in the same plane.
Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection

12.4 Important Terms

  • Incident ray: The ray travelling towards the surface.
  • Reflected ray: The ray travelling away from the surface.
  • Normal: A line perpendicular to the reflecting surface.
  • Angle of incidence: The angle between the incident ray and normal.
  • Angle of reflection: The angle between the reflected ray and normal.

12.5 Regular Reflection

Regular reflection occurs when parallel light rays strike a smooth surface and are reflected in a regular direction.

12.6 Diffuse Reflection

Diffuse reflection occurs when light strikes a rough surface and is reflected in different directions.

12.7 Plane Mirror

A plane mirror is a flat reflecting surface.

12.8 Characteristics of the Image in a Plane Mirror

  • Virtual
  • Upright
  • Same size as the object
  • Laterally inverted
  • Same distance behind the mirror as the object is in front

12.9 Applications of Reflection

  • Periscopes
  • Rear-view mirrors
  • Solar reflectors
  • Optical instruments
  • Decorative mirrors

Exercise

Unit 13: Telecommunications Channel

13.1 Telecommunication

Telecommunication is the transmission of information over a distance using electrical, electromagnetic or optical signals.

13.2 Information Signals

Information such as voice, images, text and data can be converted into signals suitable for transmission.

13.3 Communication System

A basic communication system consists of:

  • Information source
  • Transmitter
  • Communication channel
  • Receiver
  • Destination

13.4 Telecommunications Channel

A telecommunications channel is the path or medium through which information signals travel from a transmitter to a receiver.

13.5 Types of Transmission Media

Guided Media

  • Twisted-pair cable
  • Coaxial cable
  • Optical fibre

Unguided Media

  • Radio waves
  • Microwaves
  • Satellite communication

13.6 Optical Fibre

An optical fibre is a thin transparent fibre that carries information using pulses of light.

13.7 Advantages of Optical Fibre

  • High bandwidth
  • Low signal loss over suitable distances
  • Resistance to electromagnetic interference
  • Light weight
  • Can carry large amounts of information

13.8 Radio Communication

Radio communication uses electromagnetic waves to transmit information through space.

13.9 Modulation

Modulation is the process of varying a property of a carrier wave according to the information signal.

Common forms include amplitude modulation (AM) and frequency modulation (FM).

13.10 Applications of Telecommunication

  • Telephone communication
  • Radio
  • Television
  • Internet communication
  • Satellite communication
  • Mobile communication
