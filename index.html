📖 Unit 1: Chemistry of Science

1. Meaning of Chemistry

Chemistry is the branch of science that deals with the study of matter, its composition, properties, structure and the changes it undergoes.

2. Relationship Between Chemistry and Other Sciences

Chemistry is connected with other branches of science because chemical processes occur in many areas.

  • Physics: Chemistry uses physical principles such as energy, temperature and forces.
  • Biology: Chemistry helps to understand living organisms, digestion and medicines.
  • Geology: Chemistry helps in studying minerals and rocks.
  • Environmental science: Chemistry helps to understand pollution and environmental protection.

3. Importance of Chemistry in Daily Life

  • Making medicines and vaccines.
  • Production of fertilizers for agriculture.
  • Manufacturing soaps and detergents.
  • Food preservation and processing.
  • Production of fuels and plastics.
  • Water treatment and purification.

4. Branches of Chemistry

(a) Organic Chemistry

Study of compounds containing carbon, especially hydrocarbons.

(b) Inorganic Chemistry

Study of substances that are mainly not carbon compounds.

(c) Physical Chemistry

Study of energy changes and the physical principles involved in chemical reactions.

(d) Analytical Chemistry

Study of identifying and measuring chemical substances.

(e) Biochemistry

Study of chemical processes that occur in living organisms.

5. Scientific Method in Chemistry

Scientists use a systematic method to solve problems and discover new knowledge.

Steps of Scientific Method

  1. Observation: Noticing a scientific problem.
  2. Question: Asking what needs to be investigated.
  3. Hypothesis: Making a possible explanation.
  4. Experiment: Testing the hypothesis.
  5. Results: Recording observations and measurements.
  6. Conclusion: Making a decision based on results.

6. Chemical Investigation

A chemical investigation is an experiment carried out to study chemical properties or changes.

7. Safety During Chemical Investigations

  • Wear protective equipment.
  • Use chemicals carefully.
  • Read labels before using chemicals.
  • Keep the laboratory clean.
  • Dispose waste properly.

8. Careers Related to Chemistry

  • Pharmacist
  • Chemical engineer
  • Laboratory technician
  • Doctor
  • Environmental scientist
