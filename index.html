Unit 7: Food Nutrients and Diet

1. Meaning of Nutrients

Nutrients are substances in food needed by living organisms for energy, growth, repair and maintaining good health.

Types of Nutrients

  • Carbohydrates: Provide energy.
  • Proteins: Needed for growth and repair of body tissues.
  • Fats and oils: Provide energy and insulation.
  • Vitamins: Protect the body from diseases.
  • Minerals: Needed for body functions.
  • Water: Helps transport substances and control temperature.
  • Fibre: Helps movement of food through the gut.

Food Tests

  • Iodine test - tests for starch.
  • Benedict's test - tests for reducing sugars.
  • Biuret test - tests for proteins.
  • Ethanol emulsion test - tests for fats.

Balanced Diet

A balanced diet contains all nutrients in correct amounts required by the body.

Deficiency Diseases

  • Vitamin C deficiency - Scurvy
  • Vitamin D deficiency - Rickets
  • Iron deficiency - Anaemia
  • Protein deficiency - Kwashiorkor

Unit 8: Structure and Functions of Human Gas Exchange System

Meaning of Gas Exchange

Gas exchange is the process of taking in oxygen and removing carbon dioxide from the body.

Parts of Human Respiratory System

  • Nose
  • Trachea
  • Bronchi
  • Bronchioles
  • Lungs
  • Alveoli

Functions of Parts

  • Nose - filters and warms air.
  • Trachea - carries air to lungs.
  • Bronchi - transport air into lungs.
  • Alveoli - site of gas exchange.

Features of Alveoli

  • Large surface area.
  • Thin walls.
  • Rich blood supply.
  • Moist surface.

Breathing Process

  • Inhalation: Taking air into the lungs.
  • Exhalation: Removing air from the lungs.

Unit 9: Tropic Responses

Meaning of Tropism

A tropism is a growth response of a plant towards or away from a stimulus.

Types of Tropisms

  • Phototropism: Response to light.
    Example: Shoots grow towards light.
  • Geotropism: Response to gravity.
    Example: Roots grow downwards.
  • Hydrotropism: Response to water.
    Example: Roots grow towards water.
  • Thigmotropism: Response to touch.
    Example: Tendrils climbing around objects.

Importance of Tropisms

  • Help plants obtain light.
  • Help roots find water and minerals.
  • Help plants survive in their environment.
