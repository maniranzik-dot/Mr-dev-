📖 Unit 11: Reproduction

Meaning of reproduction

Reproduction is the process by which living organisms produce new individuals of the same species.

Importance of reproduction

  • Ensures continuity of a species.
  • Replaces organisms that die.
  • Allows transfer of genetic information.

Types of reproduction

1. Asexual reproduction

Asexual reproduction involves one parent and produces offspring that are genetically identical to the parent.

Examples:

  • Binary fission in bacteria
  • Budding in yeast
  • Vegetative reproduction in plants

2. Sexual reproduction

Sexual reproduction involves two parents and the fusion of male and female gametes.

Reproduction in flowering plants

The flower is the reproductive organ of a flowering plant.

  • Stamen: Male reproductive part.
  • Carpel: Female reproductive part.

Pollination

Pollination is the transfer of pollen grains from the anther to the stigma.

Fertilisation

Fertilisation is the fusion of male and female gametes to form a zygote.

`, example:`

💡 Example

A potato plant can reproduce asexually using its stem tubers.

📖 Unit 12: Growth and Development

Meaning of growth

Growth is the permanent increase in size, mass and number of cells of an organism.

Development

Development is the process of changes that occur as an organism becomes mature.

Cell division and growth

Growth occurs through:

  • Increase in cell size.
  • Increase in cell number through cell division.

Mitosis

Mitosis is a type of cell division that produces two identical daughter cells.

Importance of mitosis

  • Growth of organisms.
  • Repair of damaged tissues.
  • Replacement of old cells.

Factors affecting growth

  • Food availability.
  • Water supply.
  • Temperature.
  • Hormones.
  • Genetic factors.
`, example:`

💡 Example

A child grows taller because cells divide by mitosis and increase in number.
