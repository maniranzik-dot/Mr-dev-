📖 Unit 9: Respiration

Meaning of respiration

Respiration is the process by which living organisms break down food (glucose) to release energy.

Types of respiration

1. Aerobic respiration

Aerobic respiration occurs in the presence of oxygen.

Word equation:
Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy

2. Anaerobic respiration

Anaerobic respiration occurs without oxygen.

Example:
In muscles:
Glucose → Lactic acid + Energy

Importance of respiration

  • Provides energy for movement.
  • Provides energy for growth and repair.
  • Provides energy for body processes.

Factors affecting respiration

  • Temperature
  • Availability of oxygen
  • Amount of glucose
`, example:`

💡 Example

During exercise, muscles may lack enough oxygen and perform anaerobic respiration, producing lactic acid.

📖 Unit 10: Excretion and Homeostasis

Meaning of excretion

Excretion is the removal of metabolic waste products from the body.

Waste products

  • Carbon dioxide from respiration.
  • Urea from breakdown of proteins.
  • Excess water and salts.

Excretory organs in humans

  • Kidneys: Remove urea and regulate water balance.
  • Lungs: Remove carbon dioxide and water vapour.
  • Skin: Removes sweat containing water and salts.

Homeostasis

Homeostasis is the maintenance of a constant internal environment of the body.

Examples of homeostasis

  • Control of body temperature.
  • Control of blood glucose level.
  • Control of water balance.

Kidney function

The kidney filters blood and forms urine by removing wastes and excess substances.

`, example:`

💡 Example

When the body temperature rises, sweating helps cool the body and maintain normal temperature.
