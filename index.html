UNIT 8: Transport of Water, Minerals and Organic Substances in Plants

Transport in Plants

Plants transport water, mineral salts and manufactured food materials through special tissues.

Xylem

  • Transports water and mineral ions from roots to leaves.
  • Movement is mainly upward.
  • Provides support to plants.

Phloem

  • Transports organic food (sugars) from leaves to other parts of the plant.
  • Movement is called translocation.

UNIT 9: Gaseous Exchange in Humans and Plants

Meaning of Gaseous Exchange

Gaseous exchange is the movement of oxygen and carbon dioxide between an organism and its environment.

Gaseous Exchange in Humans

It occurs in the lungs through structures called alveoli.

Adaptations of Alveoli

  • Large surface area.
  • Thin walls.
  • Rich blood supply.
  • Moist surface.

Gaseous Exchange in Plants

  • Occurs mainly through stomata.
  • Carbon dioxide enters for photosynthesis.
  • Oxygen leaves the plant.

UNIT 10: Excretion in Humans

Meaning of Excretion

Excretion is the removal of metabolic waste products from the body.

Main Excretory Organs

  • Kidneys: Remove urea, excess water and salts.
  • Lungs: Remove carbon dioxide and water vapour.
  • Skin: Removes sweat containing water and salts.
  • Liver: Produces urea from excess amino acids.

Structure of Kidney

  • Renal artery
  • Renal vein
  • Ureter
  • Nephrons
