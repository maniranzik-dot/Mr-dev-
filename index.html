📖 Unit 6: Points, Lines and Angles

Points

A point shows an exact position or location. It has no length, width or thickness.

Lines

A line is a straight path that extends infinitely in both directions.

  • Line segment: A part of a line with two endpoints.
  • Ray: A line with one endpoint extending in one direction.
  • Parallel lines: Lines that never meet.
  • Intersecting lines: Lines that meet at a point.

Angles

An angle is formed when two rays meet at a common point called the vertex.

Types of angles

  • Acute angle: Less than 90°
  • Right angle: Exactly 90°
  • Obtuse angle: Between 90° and 180°
  • Straight angle: Exactly 180°
  • Reflex angle: More than 180°

Angle relationships

Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Angles around a point add up to 360°.

`, example:`

💡 Example

One angle on a straight line is 120°. Find the other angle.

180° - 120° = 60°

Answer: 60°

📖 Unit 7: Solids

Meaning of solids

A solid is a three-dimensional object having length, width and height.

Common solids

  • Cube
  • Cuboid
  • Cylinder
  • Cone
  • Sphere
  • Pyramid

Properties of solids

Solids have:

  • Faces
  • Edges
  • Vertices

Volume

Volume is the amount of space occupied by a solid.

Surface area

Surface area is the total area covering the outside of a solid.

`, example:`

💡 Example

Volume of a cuboid:

Volume = length × width × height

If length=5cm, width=3cm, height=2cm:

V = 5 × 3 × 2 = 30cm³
