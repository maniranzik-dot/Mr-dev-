📖 Unit 2: Laboratory Safety and Apparatus

1. Chemistry Laboratory

A chemistry laboratory is a special room where chemical experiments are carried out using chemicals and apparatus.

2. Importance of Laboratory

  • To perform experiments and observe chemical changes.
  • To test properties of substances.
  • To develop scientific skills.
  • To discover new materials and processes.

3. Laboratory Safety Rules

  • Always wear a laboratory coat, goggles and gloves when necessary.
  • Do not eat or drink in the laboratory.
  • Never taste chemicals.
  • Do not smell chemicals directly; use wafting method.
  • Read chemical labels carefully before use.
  • Keep the working area clean and organized.
  • Report accidents immediately.
  • Follow instructions from the teacher.

4. Common Laboratory Apparatus and Their Uses

1. Test Tube

Used for holding and mixing small amounts of chemicals.

2. Beaker

Used for mixing, heating and storing liquids.

3. Measuring Cylinder

Used to measure accurate volumes of liquids.

4. Conical Flask

Used for mixing solutions and carrying out reactions.

5. Burette

Used to measure and deliver accurate amounts of liquids during titration.

6. Pipette

Used to transfer a fixed volume of liquid.

7. Balance

Used to measure mass of substances.

8. Thermometer

Used to measure temperature.

9. Bunsen Burner

Used as a source of heat in experiments.

10. Tripod Stand and Wire Gauze

Used to support containers during heating.

5. Laboratory Hazards

A hazard is anything that can cause harm during laboratory work.

Types of Hazards

  • Chemical hazards: Harm caused by dangerous chemicals.
  • Fire hazards: Accidents caused by flames or flammable substances.
  • Glass hazards: Injuries caused by broken glass apparatus.
  • Biological hazards: Harm caused by microorganisms.

6. Chemical Hazard Symbols

  • Flammable - substances that catch fire easily.
  • Corrosive - substances that destroy materials or skin.
  • Toxic - substances that can poison living things.
  • Explosive - substances that may explode.
  • Oxidizing - substances that support burning.

7. First Aid in Laboratory

  • Wash chemical spills with plenty of water.
  • Report burns and injuries immediately.
  • Use first aid equipment correctly.
  • Seek medical help when necessary.

8. Laboratory Waste Disposal

Chemical waste should be disposed of properly to protect people and the environment.
