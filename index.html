Unit 1: Introduction to Biology

Meaning of Biology: Biology is the study of living organisms and their interactions with the environment.

Branches of Biology

  • Botany - study of plants
  • Zoology - study of animals
  • Microbiology - study of microorganisms
  • Ecology - study of organisms and their environment

Characteristics of Living Things (MRS GREN)

  • Movement
  • Respiration
  • Sensitivity
  • Growth
  • Reproduction
  • Excretion
  • Nutrition

Importance of Biology

  • Understanding diseases and health
  • Improving agriculture
  • Environmental conservation
  • Medicine development

Unit 2: Introduction to Classification

Classification: The arrangement of living organisms into groups according to their similarities and differences.

Importance of Classification

  • Makes identification of organisms easier
  • Shows relationships between organisms
  • Helps scientists study organisms

Five Kingdom Classification

  • Kingdom Monera
  • Kingdom Protoctista
  • Kingdom Fungi
  • Kingdom Plantae
  • Kingdom Animalia

Taxonomic Groups

Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species

Unit 3: External Structures and Importance of Flowering Plants

Main Parts of a Flowering Plant

  • Roots: Absorb water and minerals, anchor the plant.
  • Stem: Supports the plant and transports materials.
  • Leaves: Carry out photosynthesis.
  • Flowers: Used for reproduction.
  • Fruits: Protect seeds.
  • Seeds: Develop into new plants.

Importance of Flowering Plants

  • Provide food
  • Produce oxygen
  • Provide medicine
  • Provide materials like timber and fibres
