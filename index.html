♻️ Unit 9: Waste Materials

1. Meaning of Waste

Waste is any unwanted material that is thrown away after use.

2. Types of Waste Materials

(a) Solid Waste

Waste materials that have a solid form.

Examples: Plastics, papers, bottles, metals.

(b) Liquid Waste

Waste materials in liquid form.

Examples: Sewage, industrial waste water.

(c) Gaseous Waste

Waste gases released into the atmosphere.

Examples: Carbon monoxide and smoke.

3. Sources of Waste

  • Homes
  • Industries
  • Hospitals
  • Schools
  • Agriculture

4. Effects of Poor Waste Management

  • Water pollution
  • Air pollution
  • Spread of diseases
  • Bad smell
  • Destruction of environment

5. Waste Management Methods

  • Reduce: Decreasing the amount of waste produced.
  • Reuse: Using materials again instead of throwing them away.
  • Recycle: Processing waste materials into new products.
  • Incineration: Burning waste at high temperature.
  • Landfill: Burying waste in a controlled area.

⚗️ Unit 10: Chemical Equations

1. Chemical Reaction

A chemical reaction is a process where substances change to form new substances with different properties.

2. Reactants and Products

  • Reactants: Substances that take part in a chemical reaction.
  • Products: New substances formed after a chemical reaction.

3. Signs of Chemical Reactions

  • Change in colour.
  • Production of gas.
  • Formation of a precipitate.
  • Change in temperature.
  • Production of light.

4. Chemical Equations

A chemical equation is a symbolic representation of a chemical reaction using chemical formulae.

Example:

Hydrogen + Oxygen → Water

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

5. Balancing Chemical Equations

A balanced chemical equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides of the equation.

Example:

Unbalanced:
H₂ + O₂ → H₂O

Balanced:
2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

6. Types of Chemical Reactions

  • Combination reaction: Two or more substances combine to form one product.

  • Decomposition reaction: One compound breaks down into simpler substances.

  • Displacement reaction: One element replaces another element in a compound.

  • Combustion reaction: A substance reacts with oxygen producing heat.
