📖 Unit 5: Nutrition in Plants

Meaning of nutrition

Nutrition is the process by which living organisms obtain and use food for energy, growth and repair.

Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using carbon dioxide, water, sunlight and chlorophyll.

Word equation of photosynthesis

Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

(Sunlight and chlorophyll are required)

Requirements for photosynthesis

  • Light energy
  • Chlorophyll
  • Carbon dioxide
  • Water

Importance of photosynthesis

  • Produces food for plants.
  • Provides oxygen to the atmosphere.
  • Forms the base of food chains.

Mineral nutrition in plants

Plants need mineral salts for healthy growth.

  • Nitrogen - for proteins and growth.
  • Magnesium - for chlorophyll formation.
  • Phosphorus - for root development.

💡 Example

A plant kept in darkness for many days becomes yellow because it cannot produce chlorophyll properly and cannot photosynthesise.

📖 Unit 6: Nutrition in Animals

Meaning of nutrition in animals

Animals obtain food from other organisms because they cannot make their own food.

Types of nutrients

  • Carbohydrates - provide energy.
  • Proteins - growth and repair of tissues.
  • Fats - energy storage and insulation.
  • Vitamins - protection and body functions.
  • Minerals - healthy body functions.
  • Water - transport and chemical reactions.

Balanced diet

A balanced diet contains all nutrients in correct amounts to keep the body healthy.

Digestive system

The digestive system breaks down food into small soluble substances that can be absorbed.

Main parts of digestive system

  • Mouth - chewing and saliva action.
  • Oesophagus - transports food.
  • Stomach - digestion of proteins.
  • Small intestine - absorption of nutrients.
  • Large intestine - absorption of water.
  • Anus - removal of faeces.
`, example:`

💡 Example

Protein deficiency in children can cause poor growth because proteins are needed for building body tissues.
