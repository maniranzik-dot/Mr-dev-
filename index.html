Unit 11: Electrostatics

1. Meaning of Electrostatics

Electrostatics is the study of electric charges at rest.

2. Electric Charges

There are two types of electric charges:

  • Positive charge (+)
  • Negative charge (-)

3. Properties of Electric Charges

  • Like charges repel each other.
  • Unlike charges attract each other.
  • Charges can be transferred from one object to another.

4. Charging Methods

  • Charging by friction: Transfer of electrons by rubbing two materials together.
  • Charging by conduction: Transfer of charge through direct contact.
  • Charging by induction: Charging without touching the object.

5. Conductors and Insulators

Conductors: Materials that allow electric charges to move easily.

Examples: Metals like copper and aluminium.

Insulators: Materials that do not allow charges to move freely.

Examples: Plastic, rubber, glass.

6. Uses of Electrostatics

  • Photocopiers.
  • Spray painting.
  • Air cleaners.
  • Lightning protection.

Unit 12: Current Electricity

1. Meaning of Electric Current

Electric current is the flow of electric charges through a conductor.

Formula:
Current = Charge ÷ Time

2. Sources of Electricity

  • Batteries.
  • Generators.
  • Solar cells.

3. Electric Circuit

An electric circuit is a complete path through which electric current flows.

Components of a Circuit

  • Cell or battery.
  • Connecting wires.
  • Switch.
  • Lamp or resistor.

4. Types of Circuits

Series Circuit
  • Components are connected in one path.
  • Same current flows through all components.
Parallel Circuit
  • Components have separate paths.
  • Used in homes.

5. Electrical Resistance

Resistance is the opposition to the flow of electric current.

Unit of resistance is Ohm (Ω).

6. Electrical Safety

  • Avoid touching electrical appliances with wet hands.
  • Use correct fuses.
  • Do not overload sockets.
  • Repair damaged wires.

Unit 13: Rectilinear Propagation of Light

1. Meaning of Light

Light is a form of energy that enables us to see objects.

2. Rectilinear Propagation of Light

Rectilinear propagation states that light travels in straight lines.

3. Evidence of Rectilinear Propagation

  • Formation of shadows.
  • Eclipses.
  • Pinhole camera.

4. Shadows

A shadow is a dark area formed when an opaque object blocks light.

Types of Shadows

  • Umbra: Complete shadow.
  • Penumbra: Partial shadow.

5. Reflection of Light

Reflection is the bouncing back of light when it strikes a surface.

6. Laws of Reflection

  • Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.
  • The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in the same plane.

7. Uses of Reflection

  • Mirrors.
  • Periscopes.
  • Solar cookers.
