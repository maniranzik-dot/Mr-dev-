💧 Unit 7: Water and its Composition

1. Meaning of Water

Water is a chemical substance made up of hydrogen and oxygen atoms. Its chemical formula is H₂O.

2. Sources of Water

  • Rain water
  • Rivers and lakes
  • Wells
  • Springs
  • Oceans and seas

3. Composition of Water

Water is composed of two elements:

  • Hydrogen (H)
  • Oxygen (O)

Two atoms of hydrogen combine with one atom of oxygen to form one molecule of water.

4. Physical Properties of Water

  • It is colourless.
  • It is odourless.
  • It has no taste.
  • It freezes at 0°C.
  • It boils at 100°C.
  • It is a good solvent.

5. Chemical Properties of Water

  • Water can be decomposed into hydrogen and oxygen by electrolysis.
  • Water reacts with some metals to form hydrogen gas.

6. Uses of Water

  • Drinking
  • Cooking
  • Washing
  • Agriculture
  • Industrial processes
  • Generation of electricity

7. Water Pollution

Water pollution is the contamination of water by harmful substances.

Causes:
  • Industrial waste
  • Sewage
  • Agricultural chemicals
  • Plastic waste
Prevention:
  • Treat waste before releasing it.
  • Avoid dumping rubbish into water.
  • Protect water sources.

🌍 Unit 8: Air Composition and Pollution

1. Meaning of Air

Air is a mixture of gases surrounding the Earth.

2. Composition of Air

Gas Percentage
Nitrogen About 78%
Oxygen About 21%
Carbon dioxide About 0.04%
Other gases Small amounts

3. Importance of Gases in Air

Oxygen

  • Supports breathing.
  • Supports combustion.

Nitrogen

  • Needed for plant growth.
  • Used in fertilizer production.

Carbon dioxide

  • Used by plants during photosynthesis.
  • Helps maintain Earth's temperature.

4. Air Pollution

Air pollution is the presence of harmful substances in the air.

5. Causes of Air Pollution

  • Smoke from vehicles.
  • Factory emissions.
  • Burning fuels.
  • Burning rubbish.
  • Deforestation.

6. Effects of Air Pollution

  • Respiratory diseases.
  • Global warming.
  • Acid rain.
  • Damage to plants and animals.

7. Prevention of Air Pollution

  • Plant more trees.
  • Use clean energy.
  • Control industrial emissions.
  • Avoid unnecessary burning.
