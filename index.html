🔬 Light Microscope Diagram

Parts of Microscope

  • Eyepiece lens: Used to look through and magnify the image.
  • Objective lenses: Provide different levels of magnification.
  • Stage: Holds the specimen slide.
  • Stage clips: Hold the slide in position.
  • Light source/Mirror: Provides light.
  • Coarse adjustment knob: Brings image into rough focus.
  • Fine adjustment knob: Makes image clearer.

🌱 Plant Cell Diagram

Parts and Functions

  • Cell wall: Gives support and protection.
  • Cell membrane: Controls movement of substances.
  • Nucleus: Controls cell activities.
  • Cytoplasm: Where chemical reactions occur.
  • Chloroplast: Contains chlorophyll for photosynthesis.
  • Vacuole: Contains cell sap and maintains shape.

🐾 Animal Cell Diagram

Parts and Functions

  • Cell membrane: Controls substances entering and leaving the cell.
  • Cytoplasm: Site of chemical reactions.
  • Nucleus: Controls cell activities.
  • Vacuole: Stores materials.

Difference

Plant cells have a cell wall and chloroplasts, while animal cells do not.

🫁 Human Gas Exchange System (Respiratory System)

Parts and Functions

  • Nose: Filters, warms and moistens air.
  • Trachea: Carries air to the lungs.
  • Bronchi: Branches that carry air into each lung.
  • Bronchioles: Small air passages inside lungs.
  • Lungs: Organs where gas exchange occurs.
  • Alveoli: Tiny air sacs where oxygen enters blood and carbon dioxide leaves blood.

🔬 Alveolus Diagram

Features of Alveoli

  • Large surface area for gas exchange.
  • Thin walls for easy diffusion of gases.
  • Moist surface to dissolve gases.
  • Rich blood supply.

Gas Exchange

Oxygen moves from alveoli into blood, while carbon dioxide moves from blood into alveoli to be exhaled.
