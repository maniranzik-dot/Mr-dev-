📖 Unit 5: Centre of Gravity

1. Meaning of Centre of Gravity

The centre of gravity of an object is the point through which the whole weight of the object appears to act.

If an object is supported at its centre of gravity, it can balance.

2. Centre of Gravity of Regular Objects

Regular objects have their centre of gravity at their geometric centre.

Examples:

  • A uniform ruler
