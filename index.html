UNIT 4: Active Transport

Meaning

Active transport is the movement of substances across a cell membrane from a region of low concentration to a region of high concentration using energy.

Characteristics

  • Requires energy (ATP)
  • Occurs against concentration gradient
  • Uses carrier proteins

Examples

  • Absorption of mineral ions by plant roots
  • Absorption of glucose in small intestine

UNIT 5: Identification of Food Components

Food Tests

1. Test for starch
Iodine solution turns blue-black when starch is present.

2. Test for reducing sugar
Benedict's solution gives brick-red colour after heating.

3. Test for protein
Biuret solution gives purple colour.

4. Test for fats
Ethanol emulsion test gives milky appearance.

UNIT 6: Enzymes

Meaning

Enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up chemical reactions in living organisms.

Properties of Enzymes

  • Specific to their substrates
  • Affected by temperature
  • Affected by pH
  • Remain unchanged after reactions

Examples

  • Amylase breaks starch into sugar
  • Protease breaks proteins into amino acids
  • Lipase breaks fats into fatty acids and glycerol

UNIT 7: Photosynthesis

Meaning

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using carbon dioxide, water, light energy and chlorophyll.

Word Equation

Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

Conditions Required

  • Light
  • Chlorophyll
  • Carbon dioxide
  • Water

Importance of Photosynthesis

  • Produces food
  • Releases oxygen
  • Provides energy for living organisms
