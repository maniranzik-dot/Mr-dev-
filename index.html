Unit 11: Joints and Movement

Meaning of Joint

A joint is the point where two or more bones meet.

Types of Joints

  • Fixed joint: Does not allow movement (example: skull).
  • Hinge joint: Allows movement in one direction (example: knee, elbow).
  • Ball and socket joint: Allows movement in many directions (example: shoulder).

Movement

Movement occurs when muscles contract and relax to move bones at joints.

Muscle contraction → Bone movement → Body movement

Unit 12: Infectious Diseases

Meaning

Infectious diseases are diseases caused by microorganisms that can spread from one person to another.

Causes

  • Bacteria
  • Viruses
  • Fungi
  • Protozoa

Examples

  • Malaria
  • Tuberculosis
  • Cholera
  • HIV/AIDS

Prevention

  • Good hygiene
  • Vaccination
  • Clean water
  • Control of disease vectors

Unit 13: Immunity and Vaccination

Immunity

Immunity is the ability of the body to defend itself against diseases.

Types of Immunity

  • Natural immunity
  • Artificial immunity

Vaccination

Vaccination introduces weakened or inactive microorganisms into the body to stimulate protection.

Importance

  • Prevents diseases
  • Reduces spread of infections
  • Protects communities

Unit 14: Sexual Behaviour and Sexual Response

Meaning

Sexual behaviour refers to decisions and actions related to sexual activities.

Responsible Behaviour

  • Making informed decisions
  • Avoiding risky behaviour
  • Respecting yourself and others

Factors Affecting Behaviour

  • Peer pressure
  • Culture
  • Education

Unit 15: Pregnancy Prevention

Contraception

Contraception is the prevention of pregnancy using different methods.

Methods

  • Condoms
  • Pills
  • Injectables
  • Implants
  • IUD

Importance

  • Prevents unwanted pregnancy
  • Helps family planning

Unit 16: Reducing Risk of STIs and HIV/AIDS

STIs

STIs are infections mainly transmitted through sexual contact.

Examples

  • HIV/AIDS
  • Gonorrhoea
  • Syphilis

Prevention

  • Abstinence
  • Faithfulness
  • Correct condom use
  • Regular testing
  • Avoid sharing sharp objects
Safe behaviour → Prevention → Reduced STI/HIV risk
