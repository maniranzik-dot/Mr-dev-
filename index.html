Unit 8: Statistics

Meaning of Statistics

Statistics is the study of collecting, organizing, presenting and interpreting data.

Types of Data

  • Primary data: Data collected directly by the researcher.
  • Secondary data: Data collected from existing sources.

Ways of Presenting Data

  • Tables
  • Bar charts
  • Pie charts
  • Graphs

Mean (Average)

Mean is found by adding all values and dividing by the number of values.

Formula:
Mean = Sum of values ÷ Number of values

Example

Find the mean of 4, 6, 8.
Mean = (4 + 6 + 8) ÷ 3
= 18 ÷ 3
= 6

Mode

Mode is the value that appears most frequently in a set of data.

Median

Median is the middle value when data is arranged in order.

Unit 9: Probability

Meaning of Probability

Probability is the chance that an event will happen.

Formula:
Probability = Number of favourable outcomes ÷ Total possible outcomes

Probability Scale

  • Impossible event = 0
  • Unlikely event = between 0 and 0.5
  • Equal chance = 0.5
  • Certain event = 1

Examples

A coin has two sides: Head and Tail.
Probability of getting Head:
= 1 ÷ 2
= 1/2

Exercises

  1. Find the mean of 10, 20, 30.
  2. Find the mode of 2, 3, 3, 5.
  3. A bag has 5 red balls and 5 blue balls. Find probability of picking a red ball.
