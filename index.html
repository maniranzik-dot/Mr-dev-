Unit 4: Percentage, Discount, Profit and Loss

Percentage

Percentage means a part out of 100.

Formula:
Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100%

Examples

Find 20% of 500:
= (20 ÷ 100) × 500
= 100

Discount

Discount is the reduction in the original price of an item.

Formula:
Discount = Original Price × Rate of Discount

Example

A shirt costs 10,000 Frw and has 10% discount.
Discount = 10,000 × 10/100
= 1,000 Frw
New price = 10,000 - 1,000
= 9,000 Frw

Profit and Loss

  • Profit: Selling price is greater than buying price.
  • Loss: Buying price is greater than selling price.

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price

Loss = Cost Price - Selling Price

Unit 5: Ratio and Proportions

Ratio

Ratio is a comparison between two quantities using division.

Example:
2 : 3 means 2 parts compared with 3 parts.

Simplifying Ratio

Example:
10 : 15
Divide by 5
= 2 : 3

Proportion

Proportion shows that two ratios are equal.

Example:
2 : 4 = 3 : 6

Direct Proportion

When one quantity increases, the other also increases.

Inverse Proportion

When one quantity increases, the other decreases.
