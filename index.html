Unit 3: Pressure

Pressure is force acting per unit area.

Formula:
P = F / A

  • P = Pressure
  • F = Force
  • A = Area

Unit 4: Density and Pressure in Solids and Fluids

Density is mass per unit volume.

Formula:
Density = Mass / Volume

  • Solids have fixed shape
  • Liquids flow
  • Gases fill containers
