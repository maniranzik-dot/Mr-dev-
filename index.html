📖 Unit 8: Statistics

Meaning of Statistics

Statistics is the branch of mathematics concerned with collecting, organising, presenting, analysing and interpreting data.

Data

Data is a collection of information collected for a particular purpose.

Types of data

  • Qualitative data: Data describing qualities or categories.
  • Quantitative data: Data expressed using numbers.

Collecting data

Data can be collected through:

  • Questionnaires
  • Interviews
  • Observation
  • Experiments

Measures of central tendency

Mean:

Mean = Sum of values ÷ Number of values

Median: The middle value when data is arranged in order.

Mode: The value that appears most frequently.

Data presentation

Data can be represented using:

  • Tables
  • Bar charts
  • Pie charts
  • Graphs
`, example:`

💡 Example

Find the mean of:
2, 4, 6, 8

Mean = (2+4+6+8) ÷ 4

Mean = 20 ÷ 4 = 5

📖 Unit 9: Probability

Meaning of Probability

Probability is the measure of the chance that an event will happen.

Probability formula

Probability =
Number of favourable outcomes ÷ Total number of possible outcomes

Types of events

  • Certain event: An event that must happen. Probability = 1
  • Impossible event: An event that cannot happen. Probability = 0
  • Possible event: An event that may happen.

Experimental probability

Experimental probability is found by carrying out an experiment and recording results.

Theoretical probability

Theoretical probability is calculated using possible outcomes.

`, example:`

💡 Example

A bag contains 5 red balls and 5 blue balls.

Probability of picking a red ball:

= 5 ÷ 10

= 1/2
