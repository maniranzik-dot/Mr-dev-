Unit 6: Point, Line and Angle

Points and Lines

A point shows an exact position and has no size.

A line is a straight path that extends infinitely in both directions.

Types of Lines

  • Horizontal line: A line going from left to right.
  • Vertical line: A line going up and down.
  • Parallel lines: Lines that never meet.
  • Intersecting lines: Lines that cross at one point.

Angles

An angle is formed when two lines meet at a common point called a vertex.

Types of Angles

  • Acute angle: Less than 90°
  • Right angle: Exactly 90°
  • Obtuse angle: Greater than 90° but less than 180°
  • Straight angle: Exactly 180°
  • Reflex angle: Greater than 180°

Angle Properties

Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Angles around a point add up to 360°.

Unit 7: Solid Shapes

Meaning of Solid

A solid is a three-dimensional object that has length, width and height.

Common Solid Shapes

  • Cube: Has 6 equal square faces.
  • Cuboid: Has rectangular faces.
  • Cylinder: Has two circular faces.
  • Cone: Has one circular base and one vertex.
  • Sphere: A round solid with no edges.

Properties of Solids

Faces: Flat surfaces of a solid.

Edges: Lines where two faces meet.

Vertices: Corners where edges meet.

Volume Formula

Cube:
V = side × side × side

Cuboid:
V = length × width × height

Example

A cuboid has length 5cm, width 3cm and height 2cm.
Volume = 5 × 3 × 2
= 30 cm³
