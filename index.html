📖 Unit 13: Genetics and Heredity

Meaning of genetics

Genetics is the study of inheritance and variation in living organisms.

Heredity

Heredity is the passing of characteristics from parents to offspring.

Genes and chromosomes

  • Genes are units of inheritance that control characteristics.
  • Genes are found on chromosomes inside the nucleus of cells.
  • Chromosomes are structures that carry genetic information.

Variation

Variation refers to differences between individuals of the same species.

Types of variation

  • Inherited variation: Passed from parents through genes.
  • Environmental variation: Caused by environmental factors.

Examples of inherited characteristics

  • Eye colour
  • Blood group
  • Natural hair type

Examples of environmental characteristics

  • Body weight affected by diet.
  • Skin colour affected by sunlight exposure.
`, example:`

💡 Example

Children may look like their parents because they inherit genes from both parents.

📖 Unit 14: Ecology and Environment

Meaning of ecology

Ecology is the study of relationships between living organisms and their environment.

Environment

The environment consists of all factors that affect living organisms.

Components of environment

Biotic factors

These are living components.

  • Plants
  • Animals
  • Microorganisms

Abiotic factors

These are non-living components.

  • Water
  • Temperature
  • Light
  • Soil
  • Air

Food chains

A food chain shows the transfer of energy from one organism to another.

Producers and consumers

  • Producers make their own food, for example green plants.
  • Consumers obtain food by eating other organisms.
  • Decomposers break down dead organisms.

Conservation of environment

  • Planting trees.
  • Reducing pollution.
  • Protecting wildlife.
`, example:`

💡 Example

Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake

This is an example of a food chain.
