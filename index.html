📖 Unit 2: Sets of Numbers

Meaning of numbers

Numbers are symbols used to count, measure and solve problems. Numbers are grouped into different sets.

Types of numbers

  • Natural numbers (N): 1,2,3,4,5...
  • Whole numbers: 0,1,2,3,4...
  • Integers (Z): ...,-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3...
  • Rational numbers (Q): Numbers that can be written as a/b where b ≠ 0.
  • Irrational numbers: Numbers that cannot be written as simple fractions. Example: √2, π
  • Real numbers: All rational and irrational numbers together.

Number line

Numbers can be represented on a number line. Positive numbers are on the right side of zero and negative numbers are on the left side.

Operations on numbers

The four basic operations are:

  • Addition
  • Subtraction
  • Multiplication
  • Division

📖 Unit 3: Linear Functions, Equations and Inequalities

Linear equation

A linear equation is an equation where the highest power of the variable is one.

General form:
ax + b = 0

Solving linear equations

To solve an equation, make the unknown variable alone on one side.

Linear inequalities

An inequality compares two values using symbols:

  • > Greater than
  • < Less than
  • ≥ Greater than or equal to
  • ≤ Less than or equal to

Functions

A function shows a relationship between input values and output values.
