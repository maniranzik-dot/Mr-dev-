Unit 1: Chemistry of Science

1. Meaning of Chemistry

Chemistry is the branch of science that deals with the study of matter, its composition, properties, changes and reactions.

2. Importance of Chemistry

  • Used in medicine production.
  • Used in agriculture to make fertilizers and pesticides.
  • Used in industries to produce different materials.
  • Helps us understand substances around us.
  • Used in food processing and preservation.

3. Branches of Chemistry

  • Organic Chemistry: Study of carbon compounds.
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Study of substances without carbon compounds.
  • Physical Chemistry: Study of energy changes during reactions.
  • Analytical Chemistry: Identification and measurement of substances.
  • Biochemistry: Study of chemical processes in living organisms.

4. Chemistry in Daily Life

  • Cooking and food preservation.
  • Cleaning products like soaps and detergents.
  • Making clothes and plastics.
  • Water treatment.

Unit 2: Laboratory Safety and Apparatus

1. Laboratory Safety Rules

  • Always wear protective equipment like lab coat and goggles.
  • Do not eat or drink in the laboratory.
  • Follow instructions from the teacher.
  • Handle chemicals carefully.
  • Keep the laboratory clean.
  • Report accidents immediately.

2. Laboratory Apparatus and Uses

Apparatus Uses
Test tube Used for holding and heating small amounts of chemicals.
Beaker Used for mixing and measuring liquids.
Measuring cylinder Used to measure liquid volume.
Conical flask Used for mixing solutions.
Bunsen burner Used as a source of heat.
Thermometer Used to measure temperature.
Balance Used to measure mass.

3. Laboratory Safety Symbols

  • Flammable - substances that catch fire easily.
  • Toxic - substances that can poison.
  • Corrosive - substances that destroy materials.
  • Explosive - substances that can explode.
  • Irritant - substances that cause harm to skin or eyes.
