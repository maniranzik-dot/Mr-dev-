Unit 5: Centre of Gravity

1. Meaning of Centre of Gravity

The centre of gravity is the point where the whole weight of an object appears to act.

2. Importance of Centre of Gravity

  • It helps to determine the stability of objects.
  • It helps objects to balance.
  • It helps in designing vehicles and buildings.

3. Stability of Objects

Stability is the ability of an object to return to its original position after being disturbed.

Factors Affecting Stability

  • Height of centre of gravity.
  • Size of the base area.
  • Position of the object.

Types of Equilibrium

  • Stable equilibrium: An object returns to its original position after being moved.
  • Unstable equilibrium: An object moves further away after being disturbed.
  • Neutral equilibrium: An object stays in its new position.

Unit 6: Work, Power and Energy

1. Work

Work is done when a force causes an object to move through a distance in the direction of the force.

Formula: Work = Force × Distance

  • SI unit of work is Joule (J).

2. Energy

Energy is the ability to do work.

Forms of Energy

  • Kinetic energy - energy due to motion.
  • Potential energy - energy due to position or height.
  • Heat energy.
  • Electrical energy.
  • Chemical energy.
  • Light energy.

3. Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by a moving object.

Formula: KE = 1/2 mv²

4. Potential Energy

Potential energy is the energy stored in an object because of its position.

Formula: PE = mgh

5. Power

Power is the rate at which work is done.

Formula: Power = Work ÷ Time

  • SI unit of power is Watt (W).
