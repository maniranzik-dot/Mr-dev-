📖 Unit 7: Transport in Plants

Meaning of transport

Transport is the movement of substances from one part of an organism to another.

Transport tissues in plants

  • Xylem: Transports water and mineral salts from roots to leaves.
  • Phloem: Transports manufactured food (sugars) from leaves to other parts of the plant.

Absorption of water

Plants absorb water and minerals through root hair cells.

Root hair cells

  • Have a large surface area for absorption.
  • Contain a thin wall for easy movement of water.

Transpiration

Transpiration is the loss of water vapour from plant leaves through stomata.

Factors affecting transpiration

  • Temperature
  • Wind speed
  • Humidity
  • Light intensity

Importance of transpiration

  • Helps movement of water in plants.
  • Cools the plant.
  • Helps transport mineral salts.
`, example:`

💡 Example

If a plant has damaged xylem vessels, water cannot reach the leaves properly.

📖 Unit 8: Transport in Animals

Circulatory system

The circulatory system transports substances around the body.

Main parts of circulatory system

  • Heart
  • Blood
  • Blood vessels

Blood components

  • Red blood cells: Carry oxygen using haemoglobin.
  • White blood cells: Fight diseases and infections.
  • Platelets: Help blood clotting.
  • Plasma: Transports dissolved substances.

Blood vessels

Arteries:

  • Carry blood away from the heart.
  • Have thick muscular walls.

Veins:

  • Carry blood towards the heart.
  • Have valves to prevent backflow.

Capillaries:

  • Allow exchange of substances between blood and tissues.

The heart

The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood around the body.

`, example:`

💡 Example

Red blood cells contain haemoglobin which combines with oxygen and transports it to body tissues.
