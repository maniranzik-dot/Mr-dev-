Unit 4: Effective Ways of Waste Management

Meaning of Waste Management

Waste management is the collection, treatment, recycling and safe disposal of waste materials.

Types of Waste

  • Solid waste
  • Liquid waste
  • Gaseous waste
  • Hazardous waste

Methods of Waste Management

1. Recycling

Changing waste materials into useful new products.

2. Reuse

Using materials again instead of throwing them away.

3. Composting

Conversion of organic waste into manure.

4. Incineration

Burning waste at high temperatures.

5. Landfill

Disposing waste in specially prepared areas.

Importance of Waste Management

  • Reduces pollution.
  • Protects human health.
  • Keeps the environment clean.
  • Conserves resources.

Unit 5: Categories of Chemical Reactions

Meaning of Chemical Reaction

A chemical reaction is a process where substances change to form new substances.

Types of Chemical Reactions

1. Combination Reaction

Two or more substances combine to form one product.

Example:
2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

2. Decomposition Reaction

One compound breaks down into simpler substances.

Example:
CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

3. Displacement Reaction

A more reactive element replaces another element in a compound.

Example:
Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu

4. Neutralization Reaction

An acid reacts with a base to form salt and water.

Example:
HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

Signs of Chemical Reactions

  • Colour change.
  • Gas production.
  • Temperature change.
  • Formation of precipitate.

Unit 6: Preparation of Salts and Identification of Salts

Meaning of Salt

A salt is an ionic compound formed when the hydrogen ion of an acid is replaced by a metal or ammonium ion.

Methods of Preparing Salts

1. Acid + Metal

Example:
Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂

2. Acid + Base

Example:
HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

3. Acid + Carbonate

Example:
CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂

Identification of Salts

  • Chloride ions produce white precipitate with silver nitrate.
  • Sulphate ions produce white precipitate with barium nitrate.
  • Carbonates produce carbon dioxide gas with acids.

Laboratory Tests

  • Flame test.
  • Precipitation test.
  • Gas tests.
