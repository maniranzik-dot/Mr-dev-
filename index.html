📖 Unit 12: Current Electricity

1. Meaning of Electricity

Electricity is a form of energy associated with electric charges.

2. Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

Formula: I = Q ÷ t Where:
  • I = Current (Ampere, A)
  • Q = Charge (Coulomb, C)
  • t = Time (seconds, s)

3. Electric Circuit

An electric circuit is a complete path through which electric current flows.

Components of a Circuit

  • Cell or battery - provides electrical energy.
  • Wires - provide a path for current.
  • Switch - opens or closes the circuit.
  • Lamp - converts electrical energy into light.

4. Types of Circuits

(a) Series Circuit

Components are connected one after another in a single path.

Characteristics:
  • Same current flows through all components.
  • If one component fails, the whole circuit stops working.

(b) Parallel Circuit

Components are connected in separate branches.

Characteristics:
  • Each branch receives the same voltage.
  • One component can fail while others continue working.

5. Electrical Conductors and Insulators

Conductors

Materials that allow electric current to pass through easily.

Examples:
  • Copper
  • Aluminium
  • Iron

Insulators

Materials that do not allow electric current to pass easily.

Examples:
  • Plastic
  • Rubber
  • Wood

6. Potential Difference (Voltage)

Potential difference is the energy used to move a unit charge between two points.

Formula: V = W ÷ Q Unit:

Volt (V)

7. Resistance

Resistance is the opposition to the flow of electric current.

Unit:

Ohm (Ω)

8. Ohm's Law

Ohm's law states that current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided temperature remains constant.

Formula: V = IR Where:
  • V = Voltage
  • I = Current
  • R = Resistance

9. Factors Affecting Resistance

  • Length of conductor.
  • Thickness of conductor.
  • Type of material.
  • Temperature.

10. Electrical Safety

  • Avoid touching electrical appliances with wet hands.
  • Do not overload sockets.
  • Use proper insulation.
  • Switch off electricity before repairs.

💡 Examples

1. A circuit with one path for current is a series circuit.

2. If voltage is 12 V and resistance is 4 Ω:

I = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 A

3. Copper is used for wires because it is a good conductor.
