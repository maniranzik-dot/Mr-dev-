UNIT 11: Joints and Movement

Meaning of Joint

A joint is the place where two or more bones meet.

Types of Joints

  • Fixed joint: Does not allow movement. Example: skull bones.
  • Hinge joint: Allows movement in one direction. Example: elbow and knee.
  • Ball and socket joint: Allows movement in many directions. Example: shoulder and hip.

Movement in Humans

Movement occurs through the interaction between muscles, bones and joints.

UNIT 12: Infectious Diseases

Meaning

Infectious diseases are diseases caused by microorganisms that can spread from one person to another.

Causing Organisms

  • Bacteria
  • Viruses
  • Fungi
  • Protozoa

Examples of Infectious Diseases

  • Malaria
  • Tuberculosis
  • Cholera
  • HIV/AIDS

Prevention

  • Maintaining hygiene.
  • Vaccination.
  • Safe drinking water.
  • Controlling disease vectors.

UNIT 13: Immunity and Vaccination

Meaning of Immunity

Immunity is the ability of the body to resist infection and diseases.

Types of Immunity

  • Natural immunity: Protection gained naturally after infection.
  • Artificial immunity: Protection gained through vaccination.

Vaccination

Vaccination is the introduction of weakened or dead microorganisms into the body to stimulate immunity.

Importance of Vaccines

  • Prevent diseases.
  • Reduce spread of infections.
  • Protect communities.

UNIT 15: Pregnancy Prevention

Meaning of Contraception

Contraception is the prevention of pregnancy using different methods.

Methods of Pregnancy Prevention

  • Condoms
  • Pills
  • Injectables
  • Implants
  • Intrauterine devices (IUD)

Importance

  • Prevents unwanted pregnancies.
  • Allows family planning.
  • Protects health.

UNIT 16: Reducing Risk of STIs and HIV/AIDS

Meaning of STIs

STIs are infections transmitted mainly through sexual contact.

Examples

  • HIV/AIDS
  • Gonorrhoea
  • Syphilis

Ways of Reducing Risk

  • Abstinence.
  • Faithfulness to one uninfected partner.
  • Correct condom use.
  • Regular testing.
  • Avoid sharing sharp objects.
