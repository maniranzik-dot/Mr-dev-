📖 Unit 4: Percentage, Discount, Profit and Loss

Percentage

Percentage means a number expressed out of one hundred. The symbol used is %.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Discount

Discount is the reduction of the original price of an item.

Discount = Marked Price - Selling Price

Profit

Profit occurs when the selling price is greater than the cost price.

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price

Loss

Loss occurs when the cost price is greater than the selling price.

Loss = Cost Price - Selling Price

Simple Interest

Simple interest is the money earned or paid on a principal amount.

Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100

`, example:`

💡 Example

A shirt costs 10,000 RWF and is sold for 12,000 RWF.

Profit = 12,000 - 10,000

Profit = 2,000 RWF

📖 Unit 5: Ratios and Proportions

Ratio

A ratio is a comparison of two or more quantities of the same kind.

Example:
2:3 means two parts compared with three parts.

Simplifying ratios

To simplify a ratio, divide all parts by their common factor.

Proportion

A proportion shows that two ratios are equal.

Example:
2:4 = 1:2

Sharing in a ratio

A quantity can be divided according to a given ratio.
